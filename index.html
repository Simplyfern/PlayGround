● EDEN-9 RELOCATION DIRECTIVE — YEAR 2091

ASHTRAIL

Nine hundred sixty miles of rust, radiation, and bad decisions.

Lastlight is going dark. The water reclamation plant failed in March. The ward bosses are already selling the last clean air by the lungful.

You have a patched crawler, four people who trust you, and a rumor: Eden-9, a shielded valley past the Blackglass, where the sky doesn't taste like copper.

There is no map. There are tolls, storms, and things that used to be people. Drive carefully.

Survival briefing
  • Fuel, water, rations. Every day on the road burns all three. Buy at settlements, scavenge the ruins between them.
  • Chores cost a day. Scavenging, foraging, resting, patching the hull, and distilling water all take the whole day — but they keep you alive.
  • The crawler is a resource. A wrecked hull drinks fuel and crawls. Patch it with scrap while you still can.
  • People break. Keep morale up with full rations and rest. The sick and injured bleed health every day.
  • Reach Eden-9 with anyone still breathing to win. The score is how many of them.
